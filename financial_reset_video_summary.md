## THE END OF THE 40-YEAR INTEREST RATE CYCLE

### **Historical Context (1981-2021)**

For **40 years**, the world experienced a **secular decline in interest rates**:
- 1981: Fed funds rate peaked at **20%**
- 2020: Rates hit **0%** during COVID
- 2021: Rates began rising for the first time in 40 years

**This Was The Engine Of:**
- 📈 Asset price inflation (stocks, bonds, real estate)
- 🏦 Financial engineering and leverage
- 💰 Debt accumulation at all levels
- 🎯 "Buy the dip" as winning strategy

### **What Changed in 2022**

For the **first time in 40 years**, interest rates began **sustained increases**:
- Inflation returned (not transitory as claimed)
- Fed forced to raise rates aggressively
- The "Fed put" disappeared
- Asset prices declined across all categories

**This Is Structural, Not Cyclical:**
- We're not going back to 0% rates
- The 40-year bull market in bonds is OVER
- Every asset class priced for low rates is threatened
- The "rules of the game" have changed permanently

### **The Debt Spiral Problem**

**Old Regime (Low Rates):**
- Governments could borrow cheaply
- Debt service was manageable
- Growth could outpace interest costs

**New Regime (Higher Rates):**
- Borrowing costs 3-5x higher
- Interest payments crowd out other spending
- Growth slows due to debt burden
- Debt becomes self-reinforcing problem

**Mathematical Reality:**
- At 5% interest rates vs 1%
- Debt service costs increase **5x**
- With $35T debt → **$1.75 trillion** annual interest
- This exceeds defense spending + discretionary spending combined

## THE DOLLAR SYSTEM CRISIS

### **Bretton Woods II Breakdown**

**Post-WWII System (1944-1971):**
- Gold convertibility ($35/oz)
- US dollar as reserve currency
- Fixed exchange rates
- ** collapsed in 1971** (Nixon shock)

**Bretton Woods II (1971-2020):**
- Petrodollar system (oil priced in dollars)
- Dollar as unbacked reserve currency
- Floating exchange rates
- US exported inflation to world
- **Now collapsing**

### **De-Dollarization Acceleration**

**Countries Moving Away from Dollar:**
- 🇷🇺 Russia: Fully abandoned dollar system
- 🇨🇳 China: Petro-yuan system, CIPS alternative to SWIFT
- 🇮🇳 India: Rupee-ruble trade, alternative systems
- 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia: Considering pricing oil in yuan
- 🇧🇷 Brazil: Leadership in Global South alternatives

**Mechanisms:**
- Bilateral currency agreements
- Alternative payment systems (CIPS, mBridge)
- Digital currency projects (CBDCs)
- Gold repatriation
- Stock reduction of US Treasuries

**Implications:**
- Less demand for US dollars
- Higher interest rates needed to attract buyers
- Inflation risk from declining demand
- Loss of US financial hegemony
- Geopolitical realignment
